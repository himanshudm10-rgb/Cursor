Ram Doot Atulit Bal Dhama,
Anjani Putra Pavansut Nama.
This is part two of
our Hanuman Chalisa series,
where we understand the meaning
behind every line.
Tulsidas describes Hanumanji as the
son of Mata Anjani and
Vayudev,
possessing strength beyond comparison.
But to understand why he
is called Shri Ram's messenger,
we need to visit Ravan's
Lanka.
When Maa Sita was held
captive,
Hanumanji crossed the ocean,
entered Lanka,
and finally,
found her in Ashok Vatika.
He introduced himself as Shri Ram ka
doot
and gave her Shri Ram's ring.
But before returning,
Hanumanji destroyed Ashok Vatika.
Ravan sent his warriors,
including his son Akshay Kumar,
whom Hanumanji killed.
And when the rakshasas set
his tail on fire,
Hanumanji escaped and burned much
of Lanka.
The son of Vayudev had
shaken Ravan's kingdom.
Yet,
he called himself simply Bhagwan
Shri Ram ka doot.
