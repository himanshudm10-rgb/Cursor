This is part two of our Hanuman Chalisa series, where we understand the meaning behind every line. Tulsidas describes Hanumanji as the son of Mata Anjani and Vayudev, possessing strength beyond comparison. But to understand why he is called Shriram's messenger, we need to visit Ravan's Lanka. When Maa Sita was held captive, Hanumanji crossed the ocean, entered Lanka, and finally, found her in Ashokvatika. He introduced himself as Shriram's doot and gave her Shriram's ring. But before returning, Hanumanji destroyed Ashokvatika. Ravan sent his warriors, including his son Akshay Kumar, whom Hanumanji killed. And when the rakshasas set his tail on fire, Hanumanji escaped and burned much of Lanka. The son of Vayudev had shaken Ravan's kingdom. Yet, he called himself simply Bhagwan Shriram's doot.
