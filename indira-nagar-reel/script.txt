Dronacharya was so evil that he refused to teach Karna because he was a Sutaputra. But that is not the whole story. In reality, Drona never refused to teach Karna. Karna was actually Drona's student. The Mahabharata's Adi Parva lists Karna among Drona's pupils. Then why did Karna go to Parashurama? Because he wanted something Drona would not teach him, the Brahma weapon, including how to withdraw it. He wanted this to fight Arjun, but Drona refused. He said it was reserved for a Brahmin who had observed the required vows or a Kshatriya who had performed severe penance. So Karna went to Parashurama, presenting himself as a Brahmin.
